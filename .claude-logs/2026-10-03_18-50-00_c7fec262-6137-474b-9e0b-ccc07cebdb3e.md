We can — and honestly, for your constraints SD is the pragmatic pick. (It *was* in the list, just not the headline — "SDXL / SD-Turbo as fallback.") The only thing to pin down is **which** Stable Diffusion, because "SD" is a family and the right one depends on where it runs.

## "Stable Diffusion" isn't one model

| Variant | Size | Quality | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SD 1.5** | ~2 GB | dated; weak prompt adherence, bad hands/text | only if you need the smallest/oldest-compatible |
| **SDXL** | ~6.9 GB | strong, 1024px | hosted API, best SD quality/cost balance |
| **SDXL-Turbo / SD-Turbo** | ~3.5 GB | good-for-speed, 1–4 steps | **in-browser WebGPU**, or cheapest/fastest API calls |
| **SD 3.5 (L/M)** | heavy | latest SD line, excellent | hosted; note Stability Community License (free under $1M rev) |

## Why I led with FLUX.1-schnell instead

It's not that SD is wrong — it's a cost/quality/license trade:

- **FLUX.1-schnell**: Apache-2.0 (cleanest license), best prompt adherence, renders legible text, distilled/fast. Downside: 12B params → **too big for in-browser**, slightly pricier per GPU call.
- **SDXL / SDXL-Turbo**: smaller, cheaper per call, the **only realistic in-browser option**, massive ecosystem of cinematic LoRAs. Downside: a notch below FLUX on prompt-following and text.

So the rule of thumb: **in-browser or cheapest → SDXL-Turbo. Hosted and want the best look → FLUX-schnell.** Since you've committed to a real backend, the cost gap is small and both are served on the same free-tier providers — **Cloudflare Workers AI hosts SDXL, SDXL-Lightning *and* FLUX-schnell**, so you don't even have to choose at build time.

## My recommendation for Cutroom
Put **SDXL (or SDXL-Turbo for speed) as the default** image model behind your backend's `/image` endpoint, with **FLUX-schnell as a quality toggle** — both via Cloudflare Workers AI free tier, same request shape. SD is the safe, cheap default; FLUX is one env var away when you want the hero shots to pop.

## The real challenge isn't SD vs FLUX — it's **consistency**
For Story mode, the hard part is keeping the **same character/world across 5–8 scene frames**. Neither base model does that alone. You'll want, in order of effort:
1. **Lock the seed + a detailed style suffix** per story (cheap, gets you 70% there),
2. **IP-Adapter / reference image** (feed scene 1 as the style/character reference for scenes 2–N),
3. character LoRA (overkill for a demo).

That consistency work matters far more to the final feel than the SD-vs-FLUX choice.
